# Laboratory User Guide - SaaS Doctor Dentiste

## 👋 Welcome Laboratory Partner

Welcome to SaaS Doctor Dentiste! As a dental laboratory partner, you are an essential part of the dental treatment ecosystem. This platform facilitates seamless collaboration with doctors and technicians, streamlines your manufacturing workflow, and helps you deliver exceptional dental restorations efficiently.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Laboratory Setup
1. **Account Activation** - Access the platform with your laboratory credentials
2. **Laboratory Profile** - Complete your laboratory information and capabilities
3. **Service Catalog** - Define your services, capabilities, and pricing
4. **Team Setup** - Add laboratory staff members and assign roles
5. **Equipment Configuration** - Register your equipment and capabilities
6. **Quality Certifications** - Upload relevant certifications and accreditations

### Dashboard Overview
Your laboratory dashboard displays:
- **Active Orders** - Current cases assigned to your laboratory
- **Production Pipeline** - Cases in various stages of manufacturing
- **Quality Metrics** - Performance indicators and quality scores
- **Delivery Schedule** - Upcoming deliveries and deadlines
- **Resource Utilization** - Equipment and staff utilization rates
- **Financial Summary** - Revenue, costs, and profitability metrics

## 📋 Order Management

#### Order Notification Process

2. **Order Review** - Initial assessment of requirements
3. **Acceptance/Rejection** - Confirm ability to fulfill order

#### Order Information Review
Each order contains:
```

- Case Type and Specifications
- Treatment Plan Details
- Material Requirements
- Quality Standards
- Delivery Timeline
- Special Instructions
- Digital Files and References
```

### Order Processing Workflow

#### Order Status Management
Your orders progress through these stages:
1. **Received** - Order assigned to your laboratory
2. **Accepted** - Order accepted and scheduled
3. **In Production** - Manufacturing in progress
4. **Quality Check** - Final quality control
5. **Completed** - Ready for shipment
6. **Shipped** - Sent to doctor's office
7. **Delivered** - Confirmed delivery

#### Updating Order Status
Keep stakeholders informed:
1. **Navigate to Order** - Access specific order details
2. **Update Status** - Select current status from dropdown
3. **Add Progress Notes** - Explain current activities
4. **Upload Photos** - Share work-in-progress images
5. **Set Next Update** - Schedule next status update
