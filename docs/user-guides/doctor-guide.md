# Doctor User Guide - SaaS Doctor Dentiste

## 👋 Welcome Doctor

Welcome to SaaS Doctor Dentiste! This comprehensive platform is designed to streamline your dental case management, improve collaboration with laboratories and technicians, and enhance your practice efficiency. This guide will help you make the most of all available features.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Login
1. **Access the Platform** - Navigate to your practice's login URL
2. **Enter Credentials** - Use the email and password provided by your administrator
3. **Update Profile** - Complete your profile information
4. **Explore Dashboard** - Familiarize yourself with the interface
5. **Connect Google Drive** - Set up file sharing capabilities

### Dashboard Overview
Your doctor dashboard provides:
- **Active Cases** - Currently open cases requiring your attention
- **Recent Activity** - Latest updates on your cases
- **Pending Approvals** - Treatment plans awaiting your review
- **Quick Actions** - Fast access to common tasks
- **Notifications** - Important alerts and messages

## 👥 Patient Management

### Adding New Patients

#### Creating a Patient Profile
1. Navigate to **Doctor > Patients > Create New Patient**
2. Fill in patient information:
   ```
   Patient Details:
   - Full Name
   - Date of Birth
   - Contact Information (phone, email)
   - Address
   - Insurance Information
   - Medical History Notes
   - Emergency Contact
   ```

3. **Save Patient Profile** - Click "Save" to create the patient record

### Patient Privacy & Security

## 📋 Case Management

### Creating New Cases

#### Case Creation Process
1. **Select Patient** - Choose existing patient or create new one
2. **Navigate to Cases** - Go to **Doctor > Cases > Create New Case**
3. **Fill Case Details**:
   ```
   Case Information:
   - Case Type (Crown, Bridge, Implant, Denture, etc.)
   - Tooth/Teeth Numbers
   - Case Description
   - Special Instructions
   - Urgency Level (Normal, Urgent, Emergency)
   - Preferred Laboratory (optional)
   - Expected Completion Date
   ```
4. **Upload Files** - Add photos, X-rays, impressions, or other files
5. **Review & Submit** - Verify all information and submit case

#### Case Types Available
- **Crowns** - Single tooth restorations
- **Bridges** - Multi-tooth restorations
- **Implants** - Implant-supported restorations
- **Dentures** - Partial or complete dentures
- **Veneers** - Cosmetic tooth coverings
- **Orthodontics** - Orthodontic appliances
- **Repairs** - Restoration repairs and adjustments

### Case Workflow

#### Understanding Case Status
Your cases progress through these stages:
1. **Created** - Case just submitted
2. **Assigned** - Assigned to technician/laboratory
3. **In Progress** - Work has begun
4. **Treatment Plan Submitted** - Awaiting your approval
5. **Approved** - Treatment plan approved, manufacturing begins
6. **Manufacturing** - Laboratory creating the restoration
7. **Quality Check** - Final quality control
8. **Completed** - Ready for delivery
9. **Delivered** - Restoration delivered to your practice

#### Tracking Your Cases
Monitor case progress at **Doctor > Cases**:
- **All Cases** - Complete list with status indicators
- **Active Cases** - Currently in progress
- **Pending Approval** - Treatment plans requiring your review
- **Completed Cases** - Finished cases
- **Search & Filter** - Find specific cases quickly

### Communication & Updates

#### Case Comments
- **Add Comments** - Communicate with technicians and labs
- **@Mentions** - Tag specific team members
- **File Attachments** - Include additional files with comments
- **Email Notifications** - Automatic notifications for all participants

#### Status Updates
Receive notifications for:

## 📋 Treatment Plan Management

### Review Process

#### Accessing Treatment Plans
1. **Dashboard Alert** - Notification when treatment plan is ready
2. **Navigate** - Go to **Doctor > Cases > [Case ID] > Treatment Plan**
3. **Review Interface** - Comprehensive treatment plan viewer

#### Treatment Plan Components
Each plan includes:
- **Detailed Description** - Step-by-step treatment approach
- **Materials Specification** - Types and grades of materials
- **Timeline** - Estimated completion schedule
- **Cost Breakdown** - Transparent pricing information
- **Visual References** - Photos, sketches, or 3D models
- **Alternative Options** - Different treatment approaches

### Approval Process

#### Reviewing Treatment Plans
1. **Examine Details** - Carefully review all plan components
2. **Check Timeline** - Verify completion dates meet your needs
3. **Verify Costs** - Ensure pricing aligns with patient agreement
4. **Assess Quality** - Evaluate proposed materials and methods

#### Approval Options
You can:
- **✅ Approve** - Accept the treatment plan as presented
- **📝 Request Changes** - Ask for specific modifications
- **❌ Reject** - Decline and request complete revision
- **💬 Discuss** - Engage in conversation with technician

#### Revision Process
If changes are needed:
1. **Specify Requirements** - Detail exactly what needs changing
2. **Set Expectations** - Clarify timeline and cost implications
3. **Track Revisions** - Monitor revision progress
4. **Re-review** - Evaluate updated treatment plan

## 📁 File Management & Sharing

### Google Drive Integration

#### Setting Up Google Drive
1. **Connect Account** - Go to **Doctor > Google Drive**
2. **Authorize Access** - Grant necessary permissions
3. **Folder Creation** - System automatically creates organized folders
4. **Sync Settings** - Configure synchronization preferences

#### Folder Structure
Your Google Drive will be organized as:
```
/SaaS-Doctor-Dentiste/
  └── Doctor-[Your-ID]/
      ├── Cases/
      │   ├── Case-[ID]-[Patient-Name]/
      │   │   ├── Initial-Files/
      │   │   ├── Treatment-Plans/
      │   │   ├── Progress-Updates/
      │   │   └── Final-Results/
      └── Patients/
          └── [Patient-Reference]/
```

### File Upload & Management

#### Uploading Files
Multiple ways to add files:
1. **Direct Upload** - Use the web interface upload button
2. **Drag & Drop** - Simply drag files into the upload area
3. **Google Drive Sync** - Place files in appropriate Drive folders
4. **Mobile Upload** - Use mobile browser upload feature

#### Supported File Types
- **Images**: JPG, PNG, TIFF, BMP (up to 10MB each)
- **Documents**: PDF, DOC, DOCX (up to 5MB each)
- **Videos**: MP4, AVI, MOV (up to 50MB each)
- **Archives**: ZIP, RAR (up to 25MB each)
- **3D Models**: STL, PLY (up to 15MB each) - *Coming Soon*

#### File Organization Best Practices
- **Use Descriptive Names** - "Upper-Right-Molar-Crown-PreOp.jpg"
- **Include Dates** - "2024-01-15-Initial-Impression.pdf"
- **Categorize Properly** - Place files in appropriate folders
- **Add Descriptions** - Include file descriptions for clarity
