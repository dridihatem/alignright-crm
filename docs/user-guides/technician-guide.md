# Technician User Guide - SaaS Doctor Dentiste

## 👋 Welcome Technician

Welcome to SaaS Doctor Dentiste! As a dental technician, you play a crucial role in the dental treatment process. This platform is designed to streamline your workflow, improve communication with doctors and laboratories, and help you deliver exceptional treatment plans efficiently.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Initial Setup
1. **Login** - Access the platform with your provided credentials
2. **Complete Profile** - Add your skills, certifications, and experience
3. **Dashboard Tour** - Familiarize yourself with the technician interface
4. **Review Assignments** - Check your current case assignments
5. **Set Preferences** - Configure your notification and work preferences

### Dashboard Overview
Your technician dashboard displays:
- **Assigned Cases** - Cases waiting for your attention
- **Active Work** - Cases you're currently working on
- **Pending Approvals** - Treatment plans awaiting doctor approval
- **Workload Metrics** - Your current capacity and performance
- **Recent Activity** - Latest updates and communications
- **Quick Actions** - Direct access to common tasks

## 📋 Case Assignment & Management

### Understanding Case Assignments

#### How Cases Are Assigned
Cases can be assigned to you through:
- **Automatic Assignment** - System assigns based on skills and availability
- **Manual Assignment** - Admin or doctor specifically selects you
- **Self-Assignment** - You can claim available cases (if enabled)
- **Load Balancing** - System distributes work evenly among technicians

#### Case Assignment Notifications
You'll receive notifications when:
- New cases are assigned to you
- Case priorities change
- Deadlines are approaching
- Additional information is provided
- Doctors request specific modifications

### Viewing Your Cases

#### Case List Navigation
Access your cases at **Technician > Cases**:
- **All Cases** - Complete list of your assigned cases
- **Active Cases** - Currently in progress
- **Pending Review** - Awaiting your initial review
- **Submitted Plans** - Treatment plans awaiting doctor approval
- **Completed Cases** - Finished work

#### Case Details View
Each case displays:
```
Case Information:
- Patient Details (name, age, relevant medical info)
- Case Type and Specifications
- Assigned Doctor and Laboratory
- Priority Level and Deadlines
- Special Instructions
- Attached Files and Images
- Communication History
```

### Case Workflow Management

#### Case Status Progression
Your cases move through these stages:
1. **Assigned** - Case assigned to you
2. **Reviewing** - Initial case review and analysis
3. **Planning** - Creating treatment plan
4. **Submitted** - Treatment plan sent to doctor
5. **Approved** - Doctor approves your plan
6. **In Production** - Laboratory manufacturing
7. **Completed** - Case finished and delivered

#### Updating Case Status
Keep stakeholders informed by updating status:
1. **Navigate to Case** - Open the specific case
2. **Change Status** - Select appropriate status from dropdown
3. **Add Notes** - Explain status change if needed
4. **Notify Team** - System automatically notifies relevant parties

## 📋 Treatment Plan Creation

### Treatment Planning Process

#### Step 1: Case Analysis
Before creating a treatment plan:
1. **Review Patient Information** - Medical history, age, lifestyle
2. **Analyze Clinical Data** - X-rays, impressions, photos
3. **Understand Requirements** - Doctor's specifications and preferences
4. **Assess Constraints** - Timeline, budget, material preferences
5. **Identify Challenges** - Potential complications or considerations

#### Step 2: Treatment Plan Development
Create comprehensive treatment plans:
1. **Navigate** - Go to **Technician > Cases > [Case ID] > Create Treatment Plan**
2. **Plan Structure**:
   ```
   Treatment Plan Components:
   - Treatment Approach Description
   - Material Specifications
   - Step-by-Step Procedure
   - Timeline and Milestones
   - Cost Breakdown
   - Quality Specifications
   - Alternative Options
   - Risk Assessment
   ```

#### Step 3: Documentation
Include detailed documentation:
- **Technical Specifications** - Precise measurements and tolerances
- **Material Justification** - Why specific materials were chosen
- **Procedure Steps** - Detailed manufacturing process
- **Quality Standards** - Expected outcomes and quality metrics
- **Visual References** - Sketches, diagrams, or reference photos

### Treatment Plan Components

#### Material Selection
Choose appropriate materials based on:
- **Case Requirements** - Strength, aesthetics, biocompatibility
- **Patient Factors** - Allergies, preferences, budget
- **Longevity** - Expected lifespan of restoration
- **Aesthetics** - Color, translucency, surface texture
- **Cost Considerations** - Material costs and lab capabilities

#### Timeline Planning
Develop realistic timelines considering:
- **Manufacturing Complexity** - Technical difficulty level
- **Laboratory Capacity** - Current workload and equipment availability
- **Quality Control** - Time for testing and refinement
- **Shipping** - Delivery time to doctor's office
- **Buffer Time** - Allowance for unexpected issues

#### Cost Estimation
Provide accurate cost estimates including:
- **Material Costs** - Raw materials and supplies
- **Labor Costs** - Technical time and expertise
- **Equipment Usage** - Specialized equipment costs
- **Quality Control** - Testing and verification costs
- **Overhead** - Administrative and facility costs

### Quality Assurance in Planning

#### Planning Standards
Ensure your treatment plans meet:
- **Clinical Standards** - Medical and dental best practices
- **Regulatory Requirements** - FDA, CE, or local regulations
- **Laboratory Standards** - Partner lab capabilities and standards
- **Doctor Preferences** - Individual doctor's quality expectations
- **Patient Safety** - Biocompatibility and safety considerations

#### Review Checklist
Before submitting treatment plans:
- [ ] **Completeness** - All required information included
- [ ] **Accuracy** - Technical specifications verified
- [ ] **Feasibility** - Plan can be executed as described
- [ ] **Timeline** - Realistic and achievable deadlines
- [ ] **Cost** - Accurate and competitive pricing
- [ ] **Quality** - Meets or exceeds standards
- [ ] **Communication** - Clear and professional presentation

## 💬 Communication & Collaboration

### Doctor Communication

#### Effective Communication with Doctors
- **Be Professional** - Maintain professional tone and language
- **Be Specific** - Provide clear, detailed explanations
- **Be Proactive** - Anticipate questions and provide answers
- **Be Responsive** - Reply promptly to messages and requests
- **Be Educational** - Explain technical aspects in accessible terms

#### Common Communication Scenarios

##### Treatment Plan Presentation
When presenting treatment plans:
1. **Summarize Key Points** - Highlight main treatment approach
2. **Explain Material Choices** - Justify material selections
3. **Address Concerns** - Anticipate and address potential concerns
4. **Provide Options** - Offer alternatives when appropriate
5. **Set Expectations** - Clarify timeline and deliverables

##### Handling Plan Revisions
When doctors request changes:
1. **Acknowledge Request** - Confirm you understand the changes needed
2. **Assess Impact** - Evaluate effects on timeline and cost
3. **Propose Solutions** - Suggest ways to implement changes
4. **Update Timeline** - Provide revised completion estimates
5. **Document Changes** - Keep clear record of all modifications

- **Feedback Loop** - Provide feedback on completed work

## 📁 File Management

### Working with Case Files

#### File Organization
Cases typically include these file types:
- **Patient Photos** - Intraoral and extraoral images
- **X-rays** - Radiographic images
- **Impressions** - Digital scan files or photos of physical impressions
- **Specifications** - Written requirements and preferences
- **Reference Materials** - Shade guides, examples, inspirational images

#### File Review Process
1. **Download All Files** - Ensure you have complete case information
2. **Organize Locally** - Create organized folders on your computer
3. **Quality Check** - Verify file quality and completeness
4. **Request Missing Files** - Ask for additional files if needed
5. **Backup Important Files** - Keep copies of critical files

### File Upload and Sharing

#### Uploading Treatment Plan Files
Share your work by uploading:
- **Treatment Plan Documents** - Detailed written plans
- **Technical Drawings** - Sketches, diagrams, specifications
- **3D Models** - Digital models or renderings (when available)
- **Progress Photos** - Work-in-progress documentation
- **Final Presentations** - Comprehensive plan presentations

#### File Naming Conventions
Use consistent naming for easy organization:
```
Examples:
- TreatmentPlan_Case123_v1.pdf
- MaterialSpecs_Case456_Final.docx
- ProgressUpdate_Case789_2024-01-15.jpg
- Revision_Case321_v2_Comments.pdf
```

#### File Security
- **Access Control** - Files are automatically shared with case team
- **Version Control** - System tracks file versions and changes
- **Backup Protection** - Files are automatically backed up
- **Download Tracking** - System logs who accesses which files

#### Case Prioritization Matrix
Prioritize cases using:
```
Priority Levels:
1. URGENT + COMPLEX - Immediate attention required
2. URGENT + SIMPLE - Quick completion possible
3. NORMAL + COMPLEX - Requires significant time investment
4. NORMAL + SIMPLE - Standard processing
```

#### Managing Deadlines
- **Early Start** - Begin work as soon as cases are assigned

- **Regular Updates** - Keep stakeholders informed of progress
